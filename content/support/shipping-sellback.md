# Shipping & Sell Back

**Sell Back**
- Items in your inventory can be sold back for coins.
- The sell-back rate depends on the item’s stored sellBackRate.
- Default sell-back rates used in the app are **82%** for standard cases and **75%** for user-created/Case Lab cases.

**Shipping**
- Shipping can be requested from your inventory when enabled.
- Shipping can optionally cost coins (based on settings). The default shipping coin cost is **0 coins**.

Shipping carriers, delivery times, and real-world fulfillment policies are currently not specified.
