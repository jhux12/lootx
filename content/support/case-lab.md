# Case Lab

Case Lab lets you create custom cases and publish them. It is intended for experimentation and custom odds.

Key details:
- Publishing a Case Lab box may charge a one-time **publish fee** in coins (default is 0 coins unless changed in settings).
- Case Lab wins can be sold back for **75% of item value** by default (based on current settings).
- Case Lab cases are created and opened instantly after publishing.

Any additional requirements, moderation, or review steps are currently not specified.
