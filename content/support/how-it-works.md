# How Pullz.gg Works

1) **Create an account or sign in**
- You can register with email + password or sign in with Google.
- Registration requires confirming you are 18+ and accepting the terms.

2) **Add coins**
- Use the Top Up flow to select a coin package. Packages can include base coins and bonus coins.
- Payment details (pricing or conversion rates) are currently not specified.

3) **Open cases**
- Choose a case and spend coins to open it.
- The result is added to your inventory.

4) **Manage your inventory**
- You can sell items back for coins based on the item’s sell-back rate.
- You can request shipping if shipping is enabled.

5) **Provably fair outcomes**
- Case outcomes are based on a provably fair algorithm using a server seed, client seed, and nonce.

6) **Case Battles**
- Battles are listed as coming soon and are not yet available.
